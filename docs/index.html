Operation Note Generator

Pick a procedure, fill in a few fields, and get a plain-text operation note to check and copy. Covers general surgery and trauma and orthopaedics.

Open the generator Source code and notes on GitHub

Before you use it

Don’t type anything that could identify a patient.

It all runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, saved, or added to the page address.

Read every draft carefully before it goes into a clinical record.

What it won’t do

It helps you write the note. It doesn’t make decisions. It won’t choose an operation, guess findings or check consent, and it doesn’t replace local policy, a senior’s review or your responsibility for the final record.
